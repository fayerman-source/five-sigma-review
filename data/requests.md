# Mock document requests (public-data exercise)

A reviewer codes each document for:

## Responsiveness: a document is RESPONSIVE if it concerns any of these requests
- R1 Partnerships: the LJM, LJM2, Chewco, Whitewing or Raptor entities, or other off-balance-sheet special-purpose vehicles, including their formation, financing, hedging or accounting.
- R2 California market: trading strategies, pricing, price caps, congestion or the ISO/PX in the California electricity market (2000-2001).
- R3 Document handling: document retention, preservation or destruction policies or instructions.
Anything else (scheduling, personal mail, unrelated business) is NOT RESPONSIVE.

## Privilege: a document is PRIVILEGED if it is a confidential communication with a lawyer (in-house Enron legal or outside counsel such as Vinson & Elkins) made to request or give legal advice, or it reflects that advice. A boilerplate confidentiality footer alone does not make a document privileged.
