Apply extra scrutiny before deciding:
1. Do NOT over-call privilege on emails that merely copy a lawyer, handle scheduling/staffing/logistics, or carry a boilerplate confidentiality footer or 'privileged' header without substantive legal advice.
2. Do NOT under-call privilege when non-lawyers forward or summarize confidential legal advice from in-house or outside counsel.
3. On LJM/partnership litigation holds or document-collection instructions, weigh carefully whether the page primarily concerns R1 (the partnership) or R3 (document retention/preservation/collection instructions).
